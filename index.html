About

I'm originally from Vapi, Gujarat, India. Growing up by the sea, I absorbed the nature of water — depth, movement, and quiet strength. That environment shaped my ambition: to continuously evolve and contribute toward making the world better, design by design.

Engineering has always been my foundation. But my curiosity about human behavior, society, and meaning led me toward design — where logic meets empathy and systems meet people. That shift gave me a rare lens: I think in systems and I design for humans.

Currently at SAP Labs, I work on AI-enabled enterprise workflows across SAP Analytics Cloud, Datasphere, and BDC Content Network — making complex tools feel inevitable rather than overwhelming.

In my creative space, I am an artist at heart — I play video games, attend design events, spend time in nature, explore food cultures, and travel whenever I can. Beyond design, I practice yoga and explore transcendental philosophy, seeking awareness, clarity, and balance in both life and work.

AI & Data 01

Vishleshan

UX Researcher & Designer

AI data-analysis tools overwhelm non-expert teams — code-first, no guidance, weak explainability.

Problem

AI data-analysis tools make it difficult for non-expert teams to turn raw data into trustworthy, repeatable decisions — driven by code-first experiences, poor guidance, weak explainability, and workflow disruptions.

Process

  1. AI market research across verticals
  2. Comparative UX assessment of 4 tools
  3. Interaction layer analysis
  4. Gap identification & drawback documentation
  5. Design philosophy formulation
  6. Ideation & new product vision

Outcomes

  • 5 core findings: chat prevalence, explainability gaps, human-in-the-loop needs
  • Documented emerging UX patterns across AI products
  • Foundation for future Vishleshan platform design

No-Code Tools 02

Terra Studio

UX Designer

Aspiring game designers blocked by coding requirements, fragmented assets, and week-long prototyping cycles.

Timeline

Sep – Dec 2023 (3 months)

Problem

Process

Outcomes

Enterprise Cloud 03

APLM Cloud Migration

Sole UX Designer

Multiple disconnected tools for data discovery, migration planning, and disposition — no unified workflow.

Timeline

4 months — end-to-end, sole UX ownership

Problem

The organisation relied on multiple disconnected tools for data discovery, migration planning, and disposition decisions. No unified workflow, no real-time visibility, heavy manual computation.

Process

  1. Stakeholder identification & persona development
  2. Heuristic evaluation & UX audit
  3. User interviews & questionnaire analysis
  4. Journey mapping & persona finalisation
  5. Feature prioritisation workshop
  6. Wireframes with iterative stakeholder review
  7. UI component design & visual style guide
  8. Prototype + developer handoff

Outcomes

  • Single platform replacing 3+ disconnected tools
  • Real-time assessment monitoring dashboard
  • AI-bot recommendations reduce manual computation
  • Event-based audit logging for full traceability
